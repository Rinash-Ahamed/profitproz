Document purpose

This policy document sets out the principles, standards, and professional practices that guide our operations, client relationships, data handling, communication, and overall service delivery. The document also defines the responsibilities of the Company, its employees, representatives, and clients in maintaining an ethical, secure, and professional working relationship. It outlines our commitment to protecting confidential information, delivering services with due care and accuracy, maintaining clear communication, and continuously improving our processes to meet client expectations and industry standards.

All employees, consultants, partners, and clients associated with the Company are expected to understand and adhere to the applicable provisions of this policy. The Company reserves the right to review, amend, or update these policies periodically to reflect changes in business requirements, technology, legal obligations, and operational practices.

Commercial Terms

The client, {{property_name}}, agrees to pay ProfitPro a one-time commission of {{percentage}}% on the total monthly revenue generated through Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) and other online booking channels for the Property. This commission shall apply only to the properties specifically covered under this Agreement, including any additional properties that are subsequently added through mutual written consent.

The commission shall be calculated based on the monthly revenue generated from all eligible online booking platforms and shall be payable within 3 days from the date of invoice issued by ProfitPro.

Commission Calculation

Monthly Commission = (Total Monthly OTA & Online Room Revenue − OTA Commission) × Agreed Commission Percentage

For the avoidance of doubt, “Monthly OTA & Online Room Revenue” means the total room revenue generated through Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) and other online booking channels during the relevant calendar month. The commission payable to ProfitPro shall be calculated on the revenue remaining after deducting the applicable OTA commissions. Any taxes collected on behalf of the Government (including GST), cancellations, refunds, or chargebacks shall not form part of the commission calculation unless otherwise agreed in writing. ProfitPro shall raise a monthly invoice based on revenue information obtained from the Property Management System, Revenue Management System, Channel Manager, booking platforms, financial records, or any other reports mutually accepted by the parties.

Third-Party Software & Termination

Third-Party Software Charges: Any charges for third-party software or services, including Channel Manager, PMS, HMS, Booking Engine, or other technology platforms, shall be paid directly by the Client to the respective vendor. ProfitPro may assist with coordination and share the vendor’s quotation or invoice for reference but shall not collect these payments.

Termination & Outstanding Payments: Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing 30 (thirty) days’ prior written notice to the other party. The Client shall clear all outstanding commissions, service fees, and any other amounts due to ProfitPro before the effective date of termination. Any unpaid dues shall remain payable even after the Agreement is terminated.
